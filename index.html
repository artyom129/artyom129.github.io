About

I'm a Python backend developer. Most of my work sits between systems: a form and a CRM, a folder of exports and a database, a mobile app and the server that owns the rules.

I care about what happens when something breaks. A provider times out, a file arrives half-copied, two clients press "book" in the same second. That part decides whether a business can rely on the software, so that is where I spend the effort.
